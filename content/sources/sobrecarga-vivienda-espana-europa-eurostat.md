Eurostat dataset ILC_LVHO07A reports the housing-cost overburden rate by age, sex, and poverty status. This slice uses the total age, total sex, and total poverty-status categories for Spain and the European Union, measured as the percentage of the population living in households whose total housing costs exceed 40% of equivalised disposable income.

The latest comparable observations in this extract are for 2025. The API marks some earlier values as estimated; the result preserves the source period and does not treat the indicator as a complete measure of housing affordability.
