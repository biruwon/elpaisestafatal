The Ministry reports that the December 2024 IMV payroll covered 673,729 households in which 2,047,755 people lived. It states that 82.4% of titleholders were Spanish nationals and that titleholders had an average age of 45.3 years. The titleholder age is not the age of every beneficiary in those households.
