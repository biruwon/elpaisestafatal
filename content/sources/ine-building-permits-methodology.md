The INE describes a monthly, nationwide operation based on major-work building permits granted by municipalities, with municipal or finer breakdown. Listed variables include granted permits, building and dwelling characteristics, location, and the planned duration and budget of works. The listed duration is for the work itself; the operation description does not list elapsed time from permit application to decision. This source description cannot establish how many construction projects were delayed or what caused a low construction rate.
