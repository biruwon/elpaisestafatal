The INE EPA table estimates salaried workers by whether they performed paid,
unpaid, or both kinds of overtime, broken down by sex and industry. For Spain's
hospitality industry in Q3 2026 it reports, in thousands of people, 1,732.5
total salaried workers; 90.0 who did overtime; 22.4 who did only unpaid
overtime; and 5.3 who did both paid and unpaid overtime. The resulting 27,700
people with some unpaid overtime are about 30.8% of hospitality workers who
did overtime and 1.6% of all salaried workers in that industry. The
corresponding estimate was 26,800 in Q1 and 39,200 in Q2; these quarterly
estimates must not be added as unique people. This industry breakdown uses
CNAE-2025 and begins in Q1 2026. It does not identify restaurants, hotels, or
regions separately.
