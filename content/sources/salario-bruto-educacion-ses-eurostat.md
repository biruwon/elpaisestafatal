Eurostat Structure of Earnings Survey: 2022 mean gross hourly earnings in Spain by employees' highest educational attainment (ISCED 2011), all sexes, in enterprises with at least 10 employees, in NACE Rev. 2 sections B–S excluding O. This measures the education employees attained, not the qualification required by their jobs. It does not establish occupation-matched pay, net pay, individual earnings, the distribution within each group, or whether pay is adequate for a qualification. The survey's four-year periodicity and coverage are described in the [Eurostat metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/earn_ses2022_esms.htm); Spain's collection and quality are documented in the [national quality report](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/earn_ses2022_esqrs_es.htm).
