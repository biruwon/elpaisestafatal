The EPA questionnaire asks employees how many overtime hours they worked in
their main job during the reference week, separately recording paid and unpaid
hours. The table's quarterly estimates therefore describe workers reporting
overtime in the reference week used for their interview, not how many hours
each employee accumulated over the full quarter or year. The survey does not
track the same individual workers to show how often unpaid overtime recurs for
each person.
