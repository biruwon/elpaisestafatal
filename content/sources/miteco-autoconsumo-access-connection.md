MITECO states that self-consumption installations without surplus injection are exempt from grid-access and connection permits. Surplus installations of up to 15 kW also qualify for the exemption when located on serviced urbanized land as defined by planning law; other installations follow the applicable access and connection procedure. This guidance describes regulatory requirements, not actual processing times, delayed projects, abandonment rates, or project profitability.
