MITECO says the binding electricity-transmission plan approved on 20 November 2026 has €17,142 million in total planned investment to the 2030 horizon and is designed to incorporate 47 GW of new electricity demand. MITECO also says the plan seeks to integrate 154 GW of renewable capacity foreseen in the PNIEC and significantly reduce renewable curtailment. These are planning targets and projected investment, not realized expenditure or a measured total of generation lost to grid constraints.

In July 2026, MITECO said the then-current annual investment limits for transmission and distribution had proved insufficient for the needs of the energy transition and new demand-access requests. The ministry described a future investment authorization, not a comparison of realized expenditure with the growth of renewable generation or demand.
