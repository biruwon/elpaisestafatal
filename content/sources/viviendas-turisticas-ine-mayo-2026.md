The INE experimental estimate reports 341,001 tourist dwellings nationally in May 2026 and a 1.28% share of the total housing stock. The [count series](https://www.ine.es/consul/serie.do?d=true&nult=15&s=VTE71) and [share series](https://www.ine.es/consul/serie.do?L=0&d=true&s=VTE287) provide the values. The INE describes the method as web scraping of Spain's three most-used tourist-accommodation platforms, regional-rule-based selection and deduplication; since August 2023, the share denominator uses the 2021 Population and Housing Census. This is not a count of homes withdrawn from residential rent, homes currently available for rent, or licensed dwellings, and national results do not describe individual neighborhoods.
