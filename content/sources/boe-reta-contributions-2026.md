For 2026, the BOE sets the reduced RETA contribution-base band for net monthly returns up to €670 at €653.59–€718.94 per month. On the minimum base, the general listed rates total 31.5%: 28.3% common contingencies, 1.3% professional contingencies, 0.9% intergenerational equity mechanism, 0.9% cessation of activity, and 0.1% professional training. Applying those rates to the minimum base gives approximately €206.88 per month before any applicable reductions or bonuses. This is a statutory example for one band, not an assessment of an individual business's viability.
