The Ministry's October 2026 Social Security affiliation presentation charts its
published contributor-to-pensioner ratio from 2014 through 2026. The series
reports 2.24 in 2014, 2.28 in 2020, 2.45 in 2025, and 2.51 in October 2026.
It also reports the intervening annual values; the ratio fell in some years,
so the overall rise is not continuous.

This is the administration's published headcount ratio, not raw contributor
and pensioner counts, a future projection, or an actuarial balance. It does
not isolate the effect of population ageing, establish a threshold for how
many contributors are "enough," or by itself determine pension-system
sustainability.
