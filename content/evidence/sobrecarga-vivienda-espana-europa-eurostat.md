Eurostat reports a housing-cost overburden rate of 7.2% for Spain and 7.7% for the EU27 in 2025, a difference of -0.5 percentage points for Spain. In 2022, Spain was at 9.2% and the EU27 at 8.7%, showing that the relative position changed across the series rather than supporting a timeless claim that one side is always worse.

The indicator uses a 40% of equivalised disposable income threshold for total housing costs. It does not measure rent or purchase prices, overcrowding, homelessness, housing quality, local availability, or every household's experience. It also does not explain why the comparison changed.
